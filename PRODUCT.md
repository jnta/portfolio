# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and peers looking at Jônata Albuquerque's engineering portfolio.

## Product Purpose
To showcase engineering skills, experience, and blog articles in a visually compelling way.

## Brand Personality
Professional, technical, modern, and polished.

## Anti-references
Generic templates, overly cluttered dashboards, unstructured text dumps.

## Design Principles
- Show, don't tell.
- Content is king, but presentation is the crown.
- Smooth interactions and transitions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance, semantic HTML, keyboard navigable.
